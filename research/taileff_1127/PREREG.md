## Tail efficiency E (fixed now)
L = -(sum of the 5 worst daily R in the split) (tail loss; own worst days). E = (L_ref - L_cfg) / (L_ref - L_cut), where
L_ref - L_cut = p x L_ref. E >= 1 means the lever cuts the tail at least as much as a size cut with the same exposure;
E < 1 = a size cut in disguise. If p <= 0: E = +inf when L_cfg < L_ref, else E = -inf (FAIL).
