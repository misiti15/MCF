# Primary bar - one yardstick for every Primary spot (owner 2026-10-27)

Educational only - not financial advice.

Owner: "if we didn't have account allocation, half or more of the setups in Primary would not make the cut ... slowly
migrate into this ... I don't want it to seem like I am jumping ship." Every Primary spot is earned and re-earned on the
same numbers; Primary may be smaller than today. Moves stay gradual: 1-3 a week, reviewed after Friday's close.

## The bar (all accounts merged; costs in; paper fills)

| Measure | To enter / stay in Primary |
|---|---|
| Live trades | ≥ 50 preferred (≥ 30 minimum to enter) |
| Avg R after costs | > 0 |
| Avg R without its best session | > 0 (owner one-big-day rule; flag if the best day is ≥ 50% of total R) |
| Win rate vs break-even win rate | above |
| Last 4 sessions avg R | not clearly negative (no slide below its long-run average - drift watch) |
| 2-year backtest R (costs in) | not clearly negative; if negative, the live record must be large (≥ 100 trades) to overrule it |

A Primary setup that fails two or more lines for two Friday reviews in a row moves to Testing (promotion = move, nothing
deleted). A Testing setup that passes every line becomes a promotion suggestion; the owner decides each move.
