    CORE TECHNICAL INDICATORS & PARAMETER CONFIGURATIONS:
      • VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price): Applied exclusively on intraday timeframes (1-min / 5-min). Serves
        as the macro baseline equilibrium point. Bullish bias rule enforced when price trades above VWAP; bearish/
        short-only bias below VWAP.
      • Exponential Moving Averages (9 EMA, 20 EMA, 200 EMA): 9 EMA acts as dynamic trailing support for fast
        micro-pullbacks; 20 EMA acts as secondary pull-back baseline. Daily 200 EMA overlaid onto intraday charts to
        flag major institutional overhead resistance or support walls.
      • Volume Bars & Candlestick Structures: Rejects line/bar charts in favor of candlestick patterns. Uses candle
        shape (topping tails, shooting stars) combined with spikes in relative volume to identify "volume tops" and
        exhaustion points.

       ACTIONABLE TRADE EXECUTION RULES:
       • Micro-Pullback Entry: Look for low float gainers trending above VWAP. Wait for a pullback to the 9 EMA or
       VWAP equilibrium on lighter volume, entering on the first candle making a new high.
       • Volume Top Exit: Exit immediately if a new intraday high forms a long upper wick (shooting star) accompanied
       by peak volume (high volume top).
       • Holy Grail Rejection: Explicitly warns against mechanical indicator cross-over systems (e.g., 9/20 EMA
       crossovers), advocating price action context over automated lagging signals.


       COMMENT SECTION VETTING & CREDIBILITY:
       Extremely high trust. Viewers confirm the strategy's real-world applicability on high-volatility gap-and-go setups.
       Audience validates Ross's audited broker statements and appreciates the transparent warning that indicators are
       tools, not black-box signal generators.

    CORE TECHNICAL INDICATORS & PARAMETER CONFIGURATIONS:
      • Flexible & Fixed Range Volume Profile: Displays trading volume by price level rather than over time,
        surfacing institutional supply and demand nodes.
      • Point of Control (POC): The single price level with the highest traded volume during a designated session or
        accumulation structure.
      • High Volume Nodes (HVN) & Low Volume Nodes (LVN): HVNs act as market magnet/acceptance zones;
        LVNs act as rapid rejection zones where price trends fast.
      • Session VWAP Integration: Combined with Volume Profile POC clusters to confirm institutional re-entry
        triggers.

       ACTIONABLE TRADE EXECUTION RULES:
       • Volume Accumulation Setup: Identify a heavy volume accumulation area (dense Volume Profile peak) inside a
       tight consolidation before an aggressive breakout.
       • The Retest Entry: When price trends away from the accumulation zone and subsequently retraces, place limit/
       confirmation orders at the Point of Control (POC) of that accumulation block.
       • Invalidation / Stop Loss: Place stops just beyond the far edge of the High Volume Node (HVN) cluster where
       low volume (LVN) begins.


       COMMENT SECTION VETTING & CREDIBILITY:
       High credibility. Comments heavily praise the shift from traditional lagging price indicators (RSI, MACD) to Auction
       Market Theory and volume distribution. No scam flags; community feedback verifies institutional logic.

    4. The MOST POWERFUL Day Trading Indicator (Anchored VWAP)
    Creator: Jumpstart Trading | URL: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QQ56E_LXIrY | Views: High Engagement | Category:
    Institutional Microstructure


    CORE TECHNICAL INDICATORS & PARAMETER CONFIGURATIONS:
      • Anchored VWAP (AVWAP): VWAP calculated from a specific high-impact anchor point (e.g., Session Open,
        Earnings Release, FOMC statement, or High-of-Year) rather than standard midnight reset.
      • Standard Deviation Bands (+1, +2, -1, -2 SD): Used to measure mathematical overextension and fair-value
        boundaries relative to the anchor point.
      • Order Flow / Delta Integration: Evaluates buyer/seller aggressive market orders at AVWAP touchpoints.

       ACTIONABLE TRADE EXECUTION RULES:
       • Event Anchor Pullback: Anchor VWAP to the major high-volume catalyst bar (e.g., market open or gap event).
       Enter on the first retest of this AVWAP line when price bounces with positive delta.
       • Mean Reversion Fade: When price extends to the +2 Standard Deviation band relative to AVWAP on fading
       volume, execute counter-trend scalps back to the AVWAP median line.


       COMMENT SECTION VETTING & CREDIBILITY:
       Highly favorably received by quantitative and systematic traders. Audience notes that replacing static moving
       averages with event-anchored VWAP substantially reduces false breakout signals in intraday models.
