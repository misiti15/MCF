# BDI regime study 2026-10-09: a market-regime signal known at entry, used as a gate

*Educational only - not financial advice. Lab backtests on the open 2-year history only; nothing here is a live or
paper result.*

Lead's brief (2026-10-09): every study so far (2-year re-score, trend guard, stacks, Reddit 2,551 configs) shows longs
win in up sessions and shorts in down sessions (about +/-0.2-0.45R), i.e. the "edges" are market direction, which is
only known at the close. Find a signal observable at 10:00 / 10:30 / at each decision bar (no look-ahead) that
predicts the session regime or the rest-of-day direction, and test it as a GATE on the existing setups and the
Reddit base rules: longs only when the gate says up, shorts only when it says down.

**Section 1 (pre-declaration) was written before any signal or gate of this study was scored.** The only things seen
beforehand are the earlier studies' published numbers (RESCORE.md, stack1009, reddit, timeofday, trendguard NOTES).
Results go in section 2; section 1 may change only by dated amendments.

### 1.3 Signal evaluation (descriptive; decision bars 10:00 and 10:30)
For each of the 16 signals x 2 bars (32 signal configurations):
- Spearman rank correlation with ROD (directional signals) or with |ROD| (non-directional), and with med_o2c.
- Sign accuracy (directional): sign(signal - 0.5) for S1/S2, sign(signal) otherwise vs sign(ROD); base rate reported.
- Walk-forward 3-class: on each test month, the signal's tercile cut points are fitted on the previous 3 open months
  (same folds as `gates.walk_forward`, locked months excluded); predicted class = the signal's tercile; accuracy vs
  the realised session regime and vs ROD terciles (cut points of ROD fitted the same way). Chance = 1/3. Reported:
  pooled out-of-sample accuracy and the share of test months above 1/3.
- Note: S1-S7 at 10:00 contain part of the session's own open-to-close, so their link to the session regime is partly
  mechanical; ROD (from the bar on) is the clean target.
No gate is chosen from this table: the gates are fixed in 1.4 now.

### 1.4 Gates (fixed now; applied at the trade's own entry bar unless stated)
"up" allows longs, "down" allows shorts; anything else blocks the trade.
| id | gate | up | down |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 brd0 | S1 breadth at the entry bar | S1 > 0.5 | S1 < 0.5 |
| G2 brd10 | S1 with a dead zone | S1 >= 0.60 | S1 <= 0.40 |
| G3 spy0 | S4 SPY from the open at the entry bar | S4 > 0 | S4 < 0 |
| G4 spy30 | S4 with a dead zone | S4 >= +0.30% | S4 <= -0.30% |
| G5 brd1000 | S1 at 10:00, fixed for the day (entries before 10:00 dropped) | S1(10:00) > 0.5 | S1(10:00) < 0.5 |
| G6 brdWF | S1 dead zone d fitted walk-forward: d from {0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15} maximising the gated exp of that trade list on the previous 3 open months (n >= 30, else d = 0.10), applied to the next month; test months only | S1 >= 0.5 + d | S1 <= 0.5 - d |
Plateau neighbours (counted): G2 with d = 0.05 and 0.15; G4 with 0.15% and 0.50%. The plateau mean of a gate = mean
exp of its one-step neighbours (G1/G3: G2/G4 at the smallest step, i.e. d = 0.05 / 0.15%; G2: d 0.05 / 0.15; G4:
0.15% / 0.50%; G5: G1 and G2 at 10:00 is not built - G5's plateau = G1; G6: the 4 fixed d values).
Also reported for each gate (not configurations): the exp of the trades the gate blocks, and the "against" gate (longs
only when the gate says down) as a sanity check of direction.

### 1.5 Counting
Per base list: 6 gates + 4 neighbours = 10 gated configurations; 102 lists -> **1,020 gated configurations**, plus 32
signal configurations (1.3). Each gated configuration's t bar = t_required(N_lineage + 20), where N_lineage = the
lineage's earlier tries (`research/history2y/rescore.csv` tries for the live/RW setups; stack1009 120,375 for ST1-ST8;
trendguard / RW6 lineage 81,430 + its trendguard tries for RW6G1; Reddit 2,551 + 400 for the 40 Reddit rules;
setup screen 15 for orb20_a / intraday_momentum; L3 not in the list) and 20 = the current and full lists x 10 gated
configurations of this study. The whole study's count (1,052) is reported too.

### 1.6 Per gated configuration reported
n, trades/day, exp R after costs, day-clustered t vs t_required, up / flat / down exp (n), walk-forward share
(`gates.walk_forward`, locked months excluded), ex-best-day exp, busiest-day share, plateau mean, verdict
(`gates.verdict`), and the ungated base for comparison.

### 1.7 Live-probation bar (as stack1009 / reddit; all must hold)
n >= 150; exp > 0 in up AND down sessions (n >= 30 each); day-clustered t >= 2.0; walk-forward share >= 0.6;
plateau mean > 0; ex-best-day > 0; busiest session <= 10% of the trades. Passers are probation candidates, not "keep",
unless t >= t_required. For passers: a lab module that adds the gate to the setup's mask, a spec for the shared
market-context helper, and a staged (not applied) Testing YAML snippet. The locked block is not scored (the lead
does that once).

### 1.8 Live parity
S1 (breadth from the open) needs, at each bar, every universe symbol's day open and last close: the runner polls the
universe bars every minute, so it is computable live at the bar close; the lab uses the 5-minute bar close of every
adv20 >= 95M name at the same timestamp (no look-ahead: the trade enters at the close of that same bar). S4 needs
SPY's open and last price (SPY is in the universe). The live implementation is a market-context feature computed once
per bar and shared by all setups (spec in section 3 if anything passes).

## 2. Results (appended 2026-10-09 ~22:40 UTC; section 1 unchanged)

*Lab backtest on the open 2-year history only (not live, not paper). Production costs. Educational only - not
financial advice.*

### 2.1 Failures first
- **No gated configuration meets the live-probation bar (0 of 1,020).** Configurations: 1,020 gated (102 lists x 10)
  + 32 signal configurations = **1,052** for the study. No lab module, helper code or Testing YAML snippet is staged
  (section 1.7 requires a passer).
- **No signal known at 10:00 or 10:30 predicts the rest-of-day direction.** Every directional signal has a Spearman
  correlation with ROD between -0.07 and +0.09 (none with p < 0.05), sign accuracy 47-55% against a 52.6% base rate of
  up afternoons, and a walk-forward tercile accuracy of 0.31-0.39 against chance 0.33.
- **The session regime looks predictable at 10:00, but only mechanically.** Breadth at 10:00 in its top tercile is
  followed by an "up" session 61% of the time (bottom tercile: 55% "down"; walk-forward 3-class accuracy 0.50 vs 0.33).
  That is the open-to-10:00 move already inside the open-to-close label. The part a trade earns, the move after the
  bar, differs by only +0.107% vs -0.047% (mean universe ROD, top vs bottom tercile at 10:00), and by nothing at 10:30.
- **Gating makes the regime split worse, not better.** Longs allowed only when breadth > 0.5 still lose in down
  sessions, and by more (Reddit longs: down-session exp -0.283 ungated, -0.487 with G1); shorts allowed only when
  breadth < 0.5 lose more in up sessions (Reddit shorts: -0.281 -> -0.486). The trades the gate keeps on the "wrong"
  sessions are exactly the mornings that looked right and then reversed. The blocked trades and the "against" trades
  (opposite gate) have about the same expectancy as the kept ones (per-group means differ by 0.00-0.03R; for the
  live-window lab lists the blocked trades are slightly better: G1 kept +0.017 vs blocked +0.030), i.e. the gate
  carries almost no information about trade outcomes.
- Mean change in expectancy from gating: Reddit base rules +0.010 to +0.022R (G3/G4/G5: 30-37 of 40 lists improve),
  still -0.06 to -0.07R after costs; lab live-window lists -0.006 to -0.063R; lab full-day lists -0.003 to -0.051R
  (G5 +0.003). Gating also cuts trade counts to 9-53% of the base.
- Out of 1,020: exp > 0 in 208; exp > 0 in up AND down sessions (n >= 30 each) in 12; t >= 2 in 9 (all with n < 230
  or a single-regime result); n >= 150 and t >= 2 in 2; walk-forward share >= 0.6 in 91. Verdicts (`gates.verdict`):
  keep 0, rework 402, retire 617 (1 configuration has no trades). At p = 0.05 about 25 configurations would reach t >= 2 by chance.

### 2.6 What this means
- The "edge = market direction" diagnosis stands, and it cannot be fixed with a morning read of the market: the
  direction of the rest of the day is not predictable from the first hour's direction, breadth, gaps, the prior day or
  SPY's VWAP (2-year history, 426 sessions). The regime label is "known" by 10:00 only because it includes the open.
- What is predictable is the **size** of the rest-of-day move (SPY ATR%, opening range, volume): a volatility-aware
  sizing or "trade / sit out" rule is the next thing to test (it is a different hypothesis, not a direction gate):
  backlog `bdi-rg-vol-regime-sizing`.
- The fix for regime dependence is setups whose edge is not market beta: market-neutral pairs (long one name / short
  its sector ETF or SPY at the same bar), or idiosyncratic big movers (the stack1009 / RW6 shape that is positive in
  both regimes). Backlog `bdi-rg-beta-hedged`.

## 3. Live parity (for the record; nothing is staged because nothing passed)
A shared market-context feature would need, once per 1-minute poll and shared by every setup:
- per universe symbol: today's first 1-minute open (09:30 bar) and the last close -> breadth from the open (S1),
  median fromOpen (S3); VWAP needs volume per bar (the runner's raw minute bars have it; the LabStrategy frame does not);
- SPY / QQQ / IWM fromOpen, SPY VWAP and its 30-minute slope, SPY opening range in ATRs, SPY daily ATR / close;
- the prior session's universe median open-to-close (from the daily cache).
Only names with adv20 >= 95M were used in the lab (about 900 per bar); live should use the same population. Since
none of these signals predicts the rest of the day, no helper is specified further and no YAML snippet is staged.
