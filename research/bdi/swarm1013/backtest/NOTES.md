# swarm1013 rule candidates on the two-year history (E1-E6): results

*Educational only - not financial advice. These are backtests on the open two-year history: 426 sessions from
2024-10 to 2026-10-07, with the locked block 2024-11-01..2025-02-28 excluded. Production costs are in. Nothing here
is a paper or live result. The rule-19 locked block was not loaded; that look belongs to the lead.*

- Plan: `PLAN.md`, written before any scoring. No amendments.
- Code: `signals.py` -> `bars.py` -> `score.py` (+ `book.py`) -> `report.py`.
- Outputs: `results.csv` has every cell with gate columns, `finalists.csv`, `summary.json`.
- The scratch data under the session scratchpad was deleted after the run.

**Configurations tried: N = 402.** That is 378 rule cells plus 24 reference cells (B0/B1 x 12 scopes).
- By family: E1 60, E2 48, E3 84, E4 84, E5 60, E6 42.
- Try-count bar: t >= max(1.5, sqrt(2 ln 402)) = **3.46**.

## Failures first
- **No configuration passes all gates (G1-G7). Nothing goes to Primary.** Nothing is ready for the lead's
  locked-block look.
- **Two cells pass G1-G4:** E1 K25 on MF4, and E5 SECT2 on MF4. Both fail G7, because MF4 itself loses (-0.10R,
  t -3.45). E1 K25/MF4 also fails G5 (walk-forward 0.59).
- **E1 T3-a "anchor the bracket on the fill" is refuted.**
  - Pooled delta: -0.009R, t -3.28.
  - Negative on both train and valid.
  - On average the live fill is already 0.03-0.08R "toward target" (P column). The reference-anchored bracket
    therefore gives the trade a closer target and a farther stop, and moving it to the fill throws that away.
- **E2 partial exits and trails lose book-wide.**
  - P-T05: -0.014, t -3.6.
  - NT075: -0.032, t -5.9.
  - NT100: -0.028.
  - P-BAR: -0.009.
  - This repeats t6-exit-overlay / sw-w4. On RW6G1 alone, P-BAR (+0.15R) and NT100 (+0.13R) are positive on train,
    but valid is only +0.02 / +0.04, at n 101.
- **E3 index-reclaim exit fails.**
  - The `any` variants help in up sessions (+0.10R) and hurt in down sessions (-0.13 to -0.16R).
  - Net pooled: -0.02R, valid t -2.5.
  - The `aln` variants are about zero.
  - On NS2: QQQ-any -0.030, QQQ-aln -0.000.
- **E4 time stops and breakeven fail.**
  - T20/T30/T45 are flat book-wide (-0.007).
  - BE03 hurts: -0.028, t -2.7.
  - NS2 T20 is the best NS2 cell: +0.024R, t 1.01. It is positive in up sessions (+0.086) and negative in down
    sessions (-0.116), so it fails G3 and G4.
  - The one-session "+2.74R" from the swarm review does not generalise.
- **E5 correlated-lot cap fails at book level.**
  - Candidate (rho 0.35, c 2): it skips 47% of the book. Delta +0.022, t 1.64; train t 1.8, valid t 0.18.
  - Down sessions: -0.051.
  - **The skipped trades are not worse than the kept ones** (kept - skipped = -0.012R). The positive delta is
    almost exactly what a random skip of 47% of a -0.053R book gives (delta vs random = -0.003).
  - On RW6G1 the cap removes 82% of its trades, and the removed ones are its *better* trades: -0.27R.
- **E6 filters**
  - **MF5-a (fromOpen > 0 & vwapDist >= 0.10):** delta +0.027, t 3.89, so it passes G3. It fails G4 (down sessions
    -0.032) and G7 (MF5 is still -0.046R).
  - **FS30 flow-sell divergence:** no selectivity. The skipped trades are *better* than the kept ones (MF pooled
    -0.056).
  - **NS2-3D (3-day return < -10%):** reversed. NS2's washed-out names did *better* (skipped trades +0.084 vs kept
    -0.026; delta -0.014).
  - **SIR stop-inside-range:** the only family with real selectivity. Skipped trades lose:
    - NS2 15m: -0.60R, n 38.
    - RW6G1 15m: -0.95R, n 18.
    - SHORTS 15m: -0.17R.
    - But it removes only 1-5% of trades, so the deltas are tiny: SHORTS +0.009, t 1.8.

**Caveat on the pre-declared delta for skip rules (post-hoc diagnostic, not a gate change).** For a
negative-expectancy scope, any skip raises the per-trade delta, because a skipped trade scores 0 instead of its
negative R. `results.csv` therefore also reports:
- `kept_minus_skip` and its approximate t, the selectivity;
- `delta_vs_random_skip`, the delta minus what a random skip of the same share gives.

## Limitations
- **Entries:** the first signal per symbol-day per setup, the lab convention. Live same-setup re-entries and the
  lot cap are not modelled.
- **Bar resolution:** 5-minute bars (the swarm replays used 1-minute bars).
- **Spread:** the half spread is the production cost model, not quotes.
- **E5:** the book is not slot-limited, and opposite-side conflicts are ignored. Correlations zero-fill missing
  returns, and windows before the dropped block reach back across it.
- **Sector mapping:** `linkage.csv` sector ETFs, a mild look-ahead in the mapping only.
- **Universe:** survivorship as rule 19.
- **Regimes:** the up/down split is a same-day label.
