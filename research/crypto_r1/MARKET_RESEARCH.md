## 2. Documented intraday effects (evidence grade, status, data needed)
- **Volatility/volume seasonality: strong and persistent.** Vol peaks at the major stock-market opens (Eross et al. 2019, Bitstamp 5-min, [Soton](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/429352/)). Hour-of-day, day-of-week and within-hour periodicity in BTC/ETH vol and volume, partly from algos and 8h perp funding ([arXiv 2109.12142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12142)). Kraken 2016–25: **13:00–21:59 UTC carries ~50% of daily BTC variance in 2022–25** (38% in 2016–18). The peak moves with **US DST**, so anchor windows to ET, not UTC ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/crypto-never-closes-but-bitcoin-ethereum-xrp-and-solana-now-move-on-wall-street-time/)). Spot-only OK.
- **Return seasonality (time-of-day/day-of-week): weak and unstable.** Baur et al. 2019 (15M obs, 7 venues): anomalies "not persistent across time" ([UWA](https://research-repository.uwa.edu.au/en/publications/bitcoin-time-of-day-day-of-week-and-month-of-year-effects-in-retu/)). Post-2020 DoW effects differ per coin (ETH yes, BTC mixed; [MDPI 2024](https://mdpi.com/1911-8074/17/8/351)). Practitioner splits by session contradict each other (K33 2022–23 "US hours best" vs Velo "bearish after US open, bullish after 21:00 UTC close"; [Blockworks](https://www.blockworks.com/news/bitcoin-trading-activity), [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/insights/btc-and-ethereum-demonstrate-distinct-hourly-trends-around-us-market-hours/)). Treat as a filter, never a standalone edge.
- **Weekends: liquidity collapse is documented, a return effect is not.** Weekend share of BTC volume fell from 24% (2018) to 13–16% (2024). Weekday volume runs ~2x weekend, and weekend spreads widen on US venues ([The Block/Kaiko](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2024-02-27-bitcoin-weekend-trading-279353)). Hence Sunday weakness and thin-book wicks. Spot-only OK.
- **CME gap ($BTC1! Fri 17:00 ET close to Sun 18:00 ET open): folklore.** "98% fill eventually" is unsourced and the timing ranges from hours to months ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/explained/what-bitcoin-cme-gaps-are-and-how-they-influence-price-movements)). Needs CME prints, and the spot Fri-17:00-ET level is a free proxy. Test only as a Sunday-evening/Monday magnet level.
- **Intraday momentum: the best academic support.** BTC opening-session return predicts closing-session return in and out of sample, stronger on high-volume/high-vol days, driven by liquidity provision (Shen, Urquhart, Wang 2022, *Financial Review*; [Reading PDF](https://reading-9.eprints-hosting.org/100181/3/21Sep2021Bitcoin%20Intraday%20Time-Series%20Momentum.R2.pdf)). The equity original is Gao et al. (R² ~1.6%). Positive intraday jumps raise P(positive close) ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42521-024-00116-1)). Wen, Bouri et al. 2022 find both momentum and reversal, with jumps flipping the sign ([NAJEF](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/ecofin/v62y2022ics1062940822000833.html)).
- **12h session reversal.** Kraken hourly 2016–25: BTC best rule = reversal in both 12h sessions from 08:00 UTC. ETH = overnight drift plus daytime reversal ([JRFM 19(9) 692](https://www.mdpi.com/1911-8074/19/9/692)). Uses only 2 trades/day, so cost-friendly. The many-rule search raises data-snooping risk, so re-test on a holdout.
- **Short-term reversal sits in illiquid coins**, which are exactly where Alpaca costs bite. Large coins show daily momentum instead (Riksbank WP 413; [PDF](https://www.riksbank.se/globalassets/media/rapporter/working-papers/2022/no.-413-trading-volume-and-liquidity-provision-in-cryptocurreny-markets.pdf)). Cross-sectional momentum is weak after costs and time-series momentum strong (Han/Kang/Ryu, [AUT](https://acfr.aut.ac.nz/__data/assets/pdf_file/0009/918729/Time_Series_and_Cross_Sectional_Momentum_in_the_Cryptocurrency_Market_with_IA.pdf)). Hourly mean-reversion on Binance spot was unprofitable even before full costs (unvetted GitHub study).
- **BTC leads alts: real but fast.** BTC→ADA lead of 16–118 s (mean 57 s), shrinking 2019–21 ([IMFI 2023](https://doaj.org/article/29451017e29f4d308664163194b9195b)). Granger causality holds at minute frequency, **not hourly** ([De Gruyter 2022](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/snde-2022-0054/pdf)). Small caps lag more (Kurihara & Matsumoto, *APFM*). The seconds-scale lag is dead at Alpaca cost. Only a slower "alt catch-up to a BTC impulse" variant is worth testing.
- **Liquidation cascades / wick reversals.** Mechanics are well documented: 10-10-2025 saw $19B liquidated, BTC −14–15%, bid books blank, ATOM printing near zero, USDe at $0.65 on one venue ([FTI](https://www.fticonsulting.com/insights/articles/crypto-crash-october-2025-leverage-met-liquidity), [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/learn/october-10-crypto-crash-explained)). Liquidity was largely restored the same day (Lim 2026, [JFDA](https://journals.lincoln.ac.nz/index.php/JFDA/article/download/1395/965/3881)). No metric warned of all 7 Binance cascades in 2022–25 (Seuma 2026 preprint). **No rigorous post-cascade reversal study found.** Real liquidation data needs derivatives feeds (Binance/Bybit/Coinglass). Spot proxy: an n-sigma drop plus a volume spike plus a long lower wick.
- **Round numbers.** >10% of BTC prices end in "00" (clustering), but **no return pattern after round numbers** (Urquhart 2017, [Econ Letters](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:ecolet:v:159:y:2017:i:c:p:145-148)). The stop-run story is practitioner lore. Use round levels only as stop/target placement and as a sweep-and-reclaim trigger.
- **Volatility clustering: strong.** Daily GARCH persistence ~0.98. Intraday realized vol is fat-tailed with long memory, and BTC has no stock-like U-shape (Aalto thesis). Use it for **sizing and gating** (vol-target, stops in ATR); it gives no direction.
- **Macro releases.** In 30-min windows BTC is **orthogonal to all US macro news except CPI** (NY Fed SR 1052, [PDF](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr1052.pdf)). Fixed clock: CPI 08:30 ET. FOMC 14:00 ET effects are multi-day and anecdotal, so treat FOMC as a risk window.
- **Stablecoin flows.** Griffin & Shams (JF 2020) found USDT issuance after BTC drops supporting prices in 2017. Later work finds flows follow prices; only *public* mint alerts moved price in a 2022 study. Daily or slower, on-chain data needed, **no intraday edge evidence**.
- **Funding rates.** Funding (every 8h at 00/08/16 UTC on most venues) tracks the perp-spot basis (He et al., [arXiv 2212.06888](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06888)). Predictive power is loose and concentrated in extremes ([BNC](https://bravenewcoin.com/insights/testing-theory-is-perpetual-swap-funding-rate-predictive)). **Needs derivatives data**; around-settlement vol periodicity is visible in spot.
- **Needs non-Alpaca data:** funding, OI, liquidations, CME gap prints, stablecoin mint/flow, external reference price (for staleness). All are free public REST (Coinbase/Kraken spot tickers, Binance/Bybit perps) and should be logged as optional features. They are not required for v1.

## 3. Costs reality and break-even
- Round trip (RT) at Tier 1: taker/taker **50 bps + spread**, maker-in/taker-out 40 bps + ½–1 spread, maker/maker 30 bps (no spread, but fill risk/adverse selection). Realistic all-in with spread: **BTC/ETH ≈ 55–65 bps taker, ≈ 35–45 bps maker-entry; liquid alts ≈ 60–90 bps**. That is roughly 10x a liquid US stock RT on Alpaca.
- Typical move size (σ ≈ daily vol/√24 per hour; daily vol BTC ~2.5–3%, ETH ~3.5–4%, SOL/DOGE/AVAX ~4.5–6%): **BTC 30 min ≈ 0.4%, 60 min ≈ 0.55–0.6%, 120 min ≈ 0.85%**. ETH ≈ 1.3x, high-beta alts ≈ 1.7–2.2x. US-session hours run ~1.15x the average hour and weekend hours ~0.7x. Estimates from vol levels; the harness must measure them per pair.
- So **one RT taker cost ≈ one 1-hour 1σ BTC move**. Unconditional 30–120 min BTC scalps cannot survive. Rules of thumb for the harness:
  1. Keep **cost ≤ 0.25R** → stop distance ≥ 4x RT cost: **≥ ~2.4% (taker) or ≥ ~1.4–1.6% (maker entry)**. Targets ≥ 2R → **gross target moves ≥ 3–5%**.
  2. A 2.4% 1σ move takes ~(2.4/0.6)² ≈ **16 h for BTC, ~4–6 h for SOL-class alts**. Intraday crypto therefore needs **high-beta pairs, conditional high-vol moments (US open, CPI, flushes), and holds of 2–12 h**, not 30-min holds.
  3. **Maker entries are mandatory by default** (post-only limit at or inside the bid). Taker only for stop exits. 10 bps saved per side is ~0.1R at a 2% stop.
  4. Frequency: target ≤ 1–3 trades/pair/day. Fee tier 3 (10/20) needs $1M+ 30-day volume and is unrealistic early.

## 4. Crypto-specific risks → failsafes required before any live crypto (SHOCK-V spirit)
1. **Separate account and halts.** Crypto runs in its own account, with its own UTC-day **no-new-entries halt (−20R analog)**, the **−2R per-setup latch**, and **SHOCK-C**: a sharp account R drop within 2–10 min → no new entries + alert, no flatten. Equity-side halts and crypto-side halts never share state.
2. **Quote-staleness gate.** No entry if the last Alpaca quote/trade is older than N s (BTC/ETH 30 s, alts 120 s), or if the Alpaca mid deviates > 50 bps from an external reference (Coinbase/Kraken public ticker). With an open position: freeze, alert, and exit by limit only once quotes are fresh again.
3. **Spread/thin-pair gate.** Pair whitelist by measured median spread and depth. No entry if spread > max(3x its 7-day hourly median, a pair cap) or if order size > 10% of top-3-level depth. Weekend book depth is roughly half, so weekends start **off or at half size** and are always reported separately.
4. **Flash-crash guard.** If a pair (or BTC) moves > 5σ of 1-min returns or > 4% in 5 min: no new entries for 15–30 min (except an explicitly tested flush setup at capped size). **Never send a market order.** Exits use marketable limits with a collar, so Alpaca's price band plus our collar prevent fills into a wick.
5. **Stops are synthetic.** Crypto has stop_limit only (it can skip in a gap), so run a software stop monitor plus a resting stop_limit with a wide limit offset. If a stop does not fill within X s, re-quote. Never leave a position without one or the other.
6. **Stablecoin depeg guard.** If USDT/USD or USDC/USD is > 50 bps off peg: halt all pairs quoted in that coin. v1 trades **USD pairs only**.
7. **Venue outage / API.** Watchdog heartbeat 24/7. Data feed dead > 2 min → entries off. Trading API errors or 429s → token-bucket limiter under 200/min **shared with any equity bot on the same account** (another reason for separate accounts). Alerts on broker-vs-journal mismatch, **including fee-reduced coin qty**.
8. **Event clock.** CPI and FOMC (ET-anchored) are risk windows: no entries from −5 to +15 min unless the setup is the event setup. A US-DST switch test is required in every backtest.
9. **Fees in the journal.** The harness and paper journal charge 15/25 bps plus measured spread on every fill (paper may not), reconcile against `CFEE` activities, and report R net of fees only.
10. **Change freeze.** There are no "market hours", so define a weekly change window (crypto engine paused by flag, e.g. Sat 00:00–02:00 UTC) and extend the `setup-freeze` check to crypto setups.

## 6. Hand-off to the harness
- Measure first: per-pair Alpaca spread and depth by hour of week; 30/60/120-min |ret| distribution per pair; weekend vs weekday split; DST-shifted US-open windows. Use these to finalise the cost model and the pair whitelist (expected 6–12 USD pairs).
- Score every idea at **taker/taker and maker/taker fees plus measured spread**, out of sample on a locked holdout (latest 3–6 months), reporting avg R w/o best day (one-big-day check) and per-pair breakdown.
