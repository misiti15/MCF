# Post style (owner 2026-11-24)

Educational only — not financial advice.

Every post summary and picture must:
- avoid tripping Reddit's auto-filters;
- read casual, plain-English and personable, like telling a friend about it at the bar (kept clean).

## Tone

- Write like you'd talk. Short sentences, contractions, first person ("I", "we"). An honest "not great" is fine.
- No jargon without a one-line plain meaning: "R = what you risked on a trade".
- Say the bad stuff plainly too. Don't hype.
- Keep it clean: no swearing, no digs at other people's projects.
- Always include a "See previous posts for the basics" line and the "not advice" line.

## Auto-filter safety (text)

- **No links, URLs, domains or handles:** none of github, dashboard or "DM me". The owner adds links himself if a sub allows them.
- **No promo or spam phrasing:**
  - no "follow", "subscribe", "join", "sign up", "free", "guaranteed", "100%", "get rich", "signals", "alerts", "copy my trades", "make $X a day";
  - no calls to buy or sell a named ticker.
- **No dollar-return brag lines in the title.** Keep money figures modest and in context, R first.
- **Light formatting:**
  - a few bold labels and short bullets;
  - no ALL CAPS words, no strings of emojis or "!!!", no repeated hashtags;
  - no long walls of tickers.
- **Paper money:** say it's paper money once, plainly.
- **Same-looking posts:** don't repeat the exact same wording across posts.
- **Length:** stay within the word limit the owner gives.

## Auto-filter safety (pictures)

- **Nothing to read off or scan:** no URLs, usernames, account numbers, Alpaca IDs, QR codes, watermarks or logos.
- **No screenshots of broker or app screens:** build charts and tables from our own data instead.
- **Plain, readable charts:**
  - dataviz light palette (surface #fcfcfb, text #0b0b0b / #52514e, blue #2a78d6, orange #eb6834, red #e34948);
  - big enough text to read on a phone;
  - a plain-English title.
- **Footer:** a small "Educational only — not financial advice" line.
- **No "$ made" banners or money-bag style graphics.**
- **File type:** PNG, a few MB at most, about 1600–2400 px wide.

## Before sending

Re-read the summary as if you were a stranger and an auto-mod. Check that it:
- sounds like a person;
- contains no link or promo word;
- makes no hype claim;
- has nothing in the images to scan or click.
