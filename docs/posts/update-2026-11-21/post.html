Quick MCF update (weekend of 11-21)

New here? See previous posts for the basics (setups, R, the accounts).

The good stuff:

  • Primary had its first week green in both R and dollars: +4.1R / +$756 (image 1). Small, but it's a first.
  • The gap-down shorts carried it again. Friday they went 20 for 20.
  • Reps keep climbing: 6,719 live trades last week, up from 4,081 four weeks ago. About 29,600 since 10-06 (image 2).
  • 182 different setups traded last week.
  • A QA pass double-checked the whole week. It found and fixed a cost bug that made the early weeks look better than they were.

The not-so-good:

  • Since Day 1, Primary is still down in R (about −88R) but up about +$310.
  • One ETF setup bought 12 ETFs in five minutes Friday and all 12 stopped out. A "burst brake" goes live Monday so that can't happen again.

Who might be next (image 3):

  • A handful of setups in the testing accounts stay positive even after you remove their single best day. That's our "not just one lucky day" test.
  • The standouts:
    • Two more gap-down shorts.
    • A "big gainer fade" (shorting stocks up 3%+ that start to roll over).
    • A late-day short that only works when the Nasdaq is down.
  • Most of them shine under specific conditions (gap-down days, weak afternoons), not every day.
  • Their records still lean on a couple of big days (11-12 and 11-20), so nobody gets promoted yet. Next real look is mid-December.

The rules, in plain English:

  • There are a LOT of rules now. Some keep a setup from piling into trades, some stop a setup for the day if it's down 2R, some decide who moves up or down, and some protect things we know work. Each one exists because something went wrong once.

Quick facts:

  • 10 paper accounts, no real money yet.
  • Primary runs 4 setups starting Monday.
  • Every number is after trading costs.
  • Promotion = a move: a setup leaves its old account when it moves up.
  • Weak setups go to the Graveyard; nothing gets deleted.
  • Monday adds the burst brake and 2 new beginner setups. One setup also leaves Primary for more testing.
