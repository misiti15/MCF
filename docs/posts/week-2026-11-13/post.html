Our rules:

  • R: every result vs what we risked.
  • -2R daily stop per setup: one bad day, it sits out. Re-tested (79 versions, 22,543 trades): kept.
  • -20R account stop: no new trades that day (fired 11-06).
  • Costs always counted; ~$4k a position; paper before real money.
  • By the numbers: setups move up or down on their records (a move, never a copy), checked without their best day.
  • The Graveyard never deletes.
