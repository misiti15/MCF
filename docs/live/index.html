Rank = average R per trade shrunk toward 0 by trades ÷ (trades + 20), so a few lucky trades do not outrank a long steady record. R = the setup's planned risk per trade. Break-even win rate = the win rate this setup needs to make money, given its own average win and loss size; green (+points) means it wins more often than it needs to, red means its winners are too small for its win rate. Avg R is the deciding number for promotion and demotion; Confidence (t) says how sure it is (about 2 or more = solid, under 1 = could be luck). vs backtest = live avg R minus the backtest's expected R (after costs) where one exists. Last 4 = avg R over the account's last 4 sessions. Playing time = the account's verdict (silent, starved, crowded out, ok). Sim. trades / sim avg R = SIMULATED: what-if replays of the setup's signals that were not taken (blocked, skipped, shadow), with costs but no fill-realism checks; never counted in live trades, win rate, R, P/L or rank. Flags: review = 20+ trades and 0.15R or more below its backtest, or 20+ trades and negative; promising = 20+ trades, positive, at or above its backtest; needs trades = fewer than 10. Paper results; few trades prove nothing. Updated by each account's end-of-day report. Educational only — not financial advice.
